Latest changes:
- Remote live and recorded video recovers more reliably from duplicate, delayed, or first-frame failures, and active viewing or recording transfers keep the tablet connected until completion.
- Recordings in the selected archive folder are rediscovered after reinstall or re-enrollment, and downloads retain concise names without internal catalog identifiers.
- H.264 exports combine standalone SEI metadata with video access units for smooth playback in standard players such as QuickTime.
- Organization configuration import continues to support enrollment links, JSON, ZIP, and saved QR-code images.
- Tracker authorization failures distinguish re-enrollment from sign-in recovery, leave offline RID and incident-map use available, and avoid repeated notices covering QR scanning.
- Protected organization and CalTopo Teams access still requires device biometrics or passcode while remaining unlocked during continuous use and brief trusted system or browser transitions.
- Organization-authorized tablets use a unique member-and-device-model name consistently across RID2Caltopo, CalTopo markers, Tracker, and recording ownership.
- Clue snapshots prefer current validated SEI position, wait when it is not usable, follow shallow camera sightlines across terrain, and identify projection-height and DEM sources.
- Android and Apple prepare the best available USGS S1M terrain tile once tablet or aircraft coordinates are usable, without delaying normal operation and while retaining lower-resolution fallback.
- Stream pairing and thumbnail publication recover more reliably, including automatic pairing for one aircraft and one unpaired live stream and prompt metadata refresh after capture.
- Automatic closing now occurs after 30 minutes without decoded RID messages; installations using the old 120-minute default migrate automatically while custom choices remain unchanged.
- Diagnostic sharing uses UAS4SAR support, removes location from log messages, and excludes tracks unless explicitly included. Update notices advise safe continued use when a store has not yet made an update available.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android asks after re-authentication whether a matching Samsung tablet is new or the same physical device. Confirming the same tablet preserves its established name and recording history; this remains Android-only because that duplicate-installation problem has only been observed there.
- Android keeps Tracker awake during remote-video and recording-transfer requests, then begins a fresh 30-second standby interval.
- Android reinstall recovery verifies a renewed archive-folder grant, suppresses repeated prompts after cancellation or failure, and offers temporary storage while protected access remains fail-closed.
- Android Quit and RID-idle closing finish reliably even from a background timer or delayed diagnostic output.
- Apple improves WebRTC offer and ICE sequencing, Safari sign-in callback recovery, stream reoffering for a confirmed drone, stable map labels, and recording publication after file finalization.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records it as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS and remains an outstanding parity gap.
