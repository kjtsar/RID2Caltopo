Remaining Android differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files rather than Android's automatic Google Drive sync; Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose the handset Wi-Fi RSSI used by Android's controller-strength warning, so Apple provides telemetry-loss and video-health diagnostics but cannot display an equivalent controller percentage.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing when the app is locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort under Apple's background policy.
- Apple still lacks Android's advanced track/alert tuning, full Proximity Pairs diagnostics, archive cleanup panel, and AD Person Relevance model; these require Apple runtime support, not placeholder UI.
