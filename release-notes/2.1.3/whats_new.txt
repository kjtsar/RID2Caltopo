Latest changes:
- Focused live streams now offer a centerpoint elevation mode: tap near the video center to show a turquoise crosshair, MSL elevation, and the sampled DEM resolution when known.
- Fresh DJI camera direction and horizontal field of view are drawn as outlined boundary rays on the map, while RID remains the canonical aircraft position.
- Live View split mode has a directly draggable handle in portrait and landscape, snaps fully to either pane, and keeps a reachable target for reopening a collapsed pane.
- Fullscreen Live View retains picture-in-picture controls and keeps Bridge RSSI visible beside the top-right controls.
- RID telemetry can pair with an incoming drone video stream even when that stream has no DJI SEI telemetry.
- A paired video stream keeps its flight active through temporary RID gaps and reconnects; the flight ends after both video and RID have been absent for 30 seconds.
- CalTopo drone tracks retain their associated video link after the flight is archived.
- Drone Confirmation is offered once per active flight; saved and ignored decisions reset only after that flight definitively ends.
- Bridge-to-tablet strength and drone-to-bridge strength are labeled separately, and brief display-clock differences no longer make fresh Bridge RSSI flash blank.
- Tracker sign-in retries when returning from the browser even when the browser does not invoke the app callback.
- Diagnostic ZIPs include matching JSON track archives for selected days, Apple ZIP entries retain valid timestamps, and iPad packaging begins fresh whenever Send App Logs is opened.
- Map attribution wraps in narrow panes, the tablet-location antenna retains a visible outline, and iPad map reloads now show progress and completion status.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android can disable Wi-Fi Beacon and Wi-Fi NAN Remote ID discovery without disabling Bluetooth RID, DS100 bridge reception, video, or normal Wi-Fi.
- Android keeps a collapsed split handle inside system-navigation gesture edges; iOS provides the equivalent directly draggable split control.
- Android and iOS provide the same centerpoint elevation and camera-field-of-view behavior when the required RID, camera, and terrain telemetry is available.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
