Latest changes:
- Tracker authorization pauses now provide Sign in and Continue offline actions while preserving RID, video, maps, and CalTopo configuration.
- Matrice 4TD video positioning now decodes complete coordinates, remains stable across telemetry gaps and counter boundaries, and rejects positions that disagree with fresh RID.
- RID remains the canonical aircraft track while validated video telemetry improves camera direction and clue placement.
- Added an opt-in full DJI SEI diagnostic capture for controlled field testing.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android and iOS provide matching Tracker sign-in and DJI SEI diagnostic controls.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
