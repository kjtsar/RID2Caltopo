Latest changes:
- Tracker status now distinguishes an expired sign-in from required tablet re-enrollment and explains how to recover without repeatedly covering the enrollment scanner.
- Organization configuration imports accept enrollment links, JSON and ZIP files, live QR scans, and QR codes saved as images.
- Remote live and recorded video playback is more reliable, including recovery from duplicate or delayed Tracker offers.
- Active Tracker playback and recording-transfer requests keep a standalone tablet connected until the request finishes.
- Clue snapshots use validated, current video telemetry for aircraft position and will not silently fall back to older Remote ID coordinates after that source is established.
- Diagnostic sharing sends to UAS4SAR support, removes location details from log messages, and excludes aircraft tracks unless the operator explicitly includes them.

Platform-specific changes:
- These are implementation-specific repairs that bring the affected platform into parity; they are not intended differences in operator behavior.
- Some controllers deliver telemetry as separate H.264 access units. Android's archive path now repairs those recordings as Apple's path already did, so both platforms provide playable downloads with concise filenames.
- Android's standby timer now stays suspended for the complete remote-video request, matching the request-lifecycle behavior already provided on Apple.
- Apple's WebRTC path now waits for the complete offer and ICE-candidate sequence required by its media framework, matching the reliable remote-playback result already provided on Android.

Known platform differences:
- These differences remain because the operating systems expose different services or impose different restrictions.
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS. This is an outstanding parity gap, not an intended platform difference.
