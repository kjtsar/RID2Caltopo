Latest changes:
- Added low-overhead network and Remote ID diagnostics to help identify video interruptions and telemetry loss.
- Added color-coded DroneScout relay-ping signal bars and a muteable spoken warning after a sustained 25-second bridge-ping gap.
- Improved live-video startup, stream focus, grid and picture-in-picture layouts, controller guidance, frozen-frame cleanup, stall recovery, and performance diagnostics.
- Video approval now opens only when measured quality choices are ready, and remote video starts conservatively before adapting toward the selected bitrate.
- Expanded live mapping with focused-aircraft centering, stable drone markers and movement-derived course pointers, operational overlays, team-map artifacts, cached marker icons, and consistent full, split, and inset views.
- Map Folders now preserve an operator's marker and folder visibility choices during map refreshes.
- Added offline map and CalTopo packages, USGS terrain and AGL support, detailed contour and imagery handling, cache maintenance, and export/reload tools.
- Added FAA NOTAM, TFR, facility-grid, national park, refuge, wilderness, and Colorado land-restriction information with freshness and source diagnostics.
- Added telemetry-loss, altitude, and proximity warnings with spoken alerts and operator controls.
- Improved clue capture with zoom-aware imagery, gimbal and heading data, ATO/AGL and distance telemetry, and Decimal, UTM, and USNG coordinates.
- Added encrypted configuration transfer, automatic backup and restore, mutual-aid packages, secure fallback behavior, and expiring incident profiles.
- Resetting persisted state now immediately resumes required archive-folder setup, and managed enrollment refreshes both NOTAM and controlled-airspace status.
- Teams configuration, tracker credential source, and FAA proxy authorization are now labeled separately, with actionable provisioning-mismatch details.
- Replaced R2C1 organization sharing with R2C2: team settings and RID mappings travel with a signed enrollment locator, and every receiving device redeems its own tracker/FAA credential.
- Improved flight confirmation, ownership coordination, track publishing and archiving, reconnect behavior, and update advisories.
- Expanded anomaly-detection controls, guide overlays, target highlighting, troubleshooting information, and captured-stream review.

Platform-specific changes:
- iOS: Added Android-aligned Main Screen, Live View, MapPane, aircraft details, stream management, offline-map, tracker, alert, and anomaly workflows.
- iOS: Added iCloud and Files integration, Keychain-protected backup passphrases, four-stream viewing, and app-managed external-display support.
- iOS: Wi-Fi Beacon and NAN aircraft use the DS110 wireless relay into the Bluetooth Remote ID receiver.
- Android: Network snapshots include available controller Wi-Fi RSSI and channel information.
- Android: The RID-2-Caltopo credentials selector is centered in the top bar to match the iPad layout.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
