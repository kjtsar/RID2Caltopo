# Contributing to RID2Caltopo

Thank you for helping improve tools for search-and-rescue teams.

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion in this repository,
you agree that it is provided under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), in
accordance with Section 5 of that license, unless you clearly designate it in
writing as "Not a Contribution" before it is incorporated.

Only submit work that you have the right to contribute. Identify code, data,
model weights, media, or other material derived from a third party and include
the applicable copyright, license, and provenance information. Do not remove or
replace existing third-party notices.

For substantial contributions, maintainers may request a Developer Certificate
of Origin sign-off in the commit message:

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>

The sign-off states that you have the right to submit the contribution under
the project's license. See <https://developercertificate.org/>.
