Latest changes:
- Local clue reports and images now reopen reliably after restart, including files created with localized timestamps.
- Tapping overlapping clues on Apple now presents a chooser; Android clue details include an obvious close button, and the video clue-capture button has a larger reliable touch target.
- Log, GeoJSON, track, and clue-report filenames now use the same operator-local timestamp convention on Android and Apple while remaining readable by existing lookup and archive workflows.
- Offline-map preparation now remains active across pane changes and exposes recoverable progress instead of appearing to disappear.
- Paired DJI aircraft remain visible on the map and in CalTopo through weak or interrupted Remote ID reception by preferring fresh validated video telemetry and falling back to Remote ID.
- Live Feed previews refresh while a matching CalTopo LiveTrack and stream are active, and remote viewing retries once if a connection starts without delivering video.
- Video archive and playback handling now preserves frame-associated metadata and improves pacing and recovery for recorded and remote video.
- CalTopo configuration now supports Connect Keys, more resilient position reporting, and retry when converting completed LiveTracks to archived flight paths.
- The startup acknowledgment now uses the same responsibility, developer hold-harmless, and California Civil Code section 1542 waiver language as the managed-access request.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android prevents temporary render sessions from replacing operational telemetry, improves remote-video frame pacing, and confirms operator-requested exits.
- Apple keeps clue selection and details accessible when markers overlap and retains one reusable decoder and telemetry timeline per publisher.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
