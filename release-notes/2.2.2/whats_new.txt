Latest changes:
- Configured organization and CalTopo Teams access now requires device biometrics or passcode before protected maps, settings, and Tracker features are shown.
- Compatible organization-authorized tablets now receive a guaranteed-unique name derived from the authenticated team member and device model, used consistently in RID2Caltopo, CalTopo R2C markers, and r2c-tracker.
- Older organization app versions retain their existing device name and are prompted to upgrade rather than having Tracker guess their hardware model; standalone tablets continue using their local device name.
- After confirming a newly detected drone, RID2Caltopo now opens the stream-pairing prompt automatically when exactly one active aircraft and one live unpaired stream are present; ambiguous cases remain available for manual selection.
- Manually paired streams with an imperfect controller designator now publish the mapped drone identity to CalTopo so Shared Locations retain their live thumbnail and external-viewer options.
- The thumbnail refresh interval is now a persistent Video Streams setting on Android and Apple, displayed in decimal seconds and defaulting to 5.0 seconds; live streams are prioritized over older recordings, and new captures update active CalTopo thumbnail metadata without waiting for another aircraft-position update.
- Diagnostic sharing now sends to UAS4SAR support, removes location details from diagnostic log messages, and excludes aircraft track files unless the operator explicitly includes them.
- Shallow-angle clue snapshots now follow the camera sightline across descending terrain to locate distant terrain intersections instead of placing the clue near the aircraft.
- Clue snapshots use valid height above takeoff as a clearly labeled flat-ground estimate when fresh AGL is unavailable, and prevent submission when no trustworthy projection height exists.
- Submitted clue descriptions now identify the projection-height source plus the DEM source and grid resolution used, or clearly identify a flat-ground estimate when no terrain intersection is available.
- Android and Apple now proactively download the best available USGS S1M 1-meter terrain tile for the tablet's current area before aircraft activity, retain lower-resolution fallback coverage, and default offline-map terrain preparation to S1M.
- Automatic closing now occurs after 30 minutes without decoded RID messages; existing installations using the previous 120-minute default migrate automatically, while custom timeout choices remain unchanged.
- Protected organization access now remains authenticated through continuous use and departures shorter than 15 seconds, avoiding repeated prompts during brief system or browser transitions.

Platform-specific changes:
- Apple immediately reoffers the current confirmed drone for pairing if an imperfectly named stream starts after takeoff; long-press remains available as the manual fallback.
- Apple Tracker sign-in now recovers cleanly when Safari does not return an app callback, without reopening an expired one-time link, and keeps protected navigation hidden until the access check completes.
- Apple map aircraft labels now remain stable during position updates and no longer display the aircraft-reported operator point.
- Apple waits for captured video files to finish finalizing before publishing their recording links.
- Android Quit and automatic RID-idle closing now remove the app reliably even when initiated by a background timer or when diagnostic-file output is slow.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
