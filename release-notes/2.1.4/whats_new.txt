Latest changes:
- Standalone organization tablets now show Tracker verified after startup synchronization, then enter Tracker standby after 30 seconds without an active map, flight, or live stream; coordination reconnects automatically when activity begins.
- Centerpoint Elevation can now use a terrain reference. Long-press near the active crosshair to set or replace the reference, then watch signed elevation changes such as +37' REF as the camera center moves.
- Tap the centerpoint elevation label to switch between absolute MSL elevation and elevation relative to the set reference. The DEM-resolution suffix remains visible in either mode.
- The Main Screen now has a persistent Pilot Callsign field between Op Period and Coordinator. That tablet callsign automatically fills the Pilot Callsign field in Drone Confirmation after restarting the app.
- Team-drone Remote ID mappings remain tied to their configured drone/video designators when the tablet pilot callsign is used, so pilots can normally review the panel and press Save without rebuilding the mapping.
- Fresh DJI SEI telemetry from a paired stream now covers temporary RID-location or DroneScout Bridge gaps. Loss warnings automatically re-arm if SEI stops or any active flight lacks fresh paired SEI.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android's first tap on a displayed live stream now establishes explicit focus and reveals the clue-snapshot control; only a later near-center tap toggles Centerpoint Elevation.
- Android and iOS provide the same persistent pilot-callsign prefill, MSL/reference elevation display, and team-drone mapping preservation behavior.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
