Latest changes:
- Installations configured for an r2c-tracker organization or CalTopo Teams account now require the device owner to unlock RID2Caltopo at startup and after leaving the app.
- Inactive incident-map connections now retire stale R2C tablet markers after tracker contact has been absent for 60 seconds or the inactive tablet has moved more than 50 feet.
- Automatic incident-map disconnection remains blocked while a flight, recent Remote ID activity, managed video or recording transfer, or offline-map preparation is active.
- Authentication remains fail-closed when no secure device credential is configured and provides direct recovery guidance.

Platform-specific changes:
- Apple offers Face ID or Touch ID first with an explicit Use Device Passcode fallback.
- Android accepts biometrics, PIN, pattern, or password and links directly to system security settings when device security is not configured.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
