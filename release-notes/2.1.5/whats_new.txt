Latest changes:
- Nearby incident tablets now exchange fresh RID and paired DJI SEI aircraft positions whether or not the reporting tablet owns or confirms the drone.
- Peer aircraft remain visible on the Map Pane with direction of travel and reported speed when available. The map automatically uses the freshest local or relayed position without waiting for an older RID track to expire. Peer positions expire after 30 seconds and do not create or publish a local flight track.
- Peer traffic is limited to tablets within one mile of the aircraft. Reports are requested about once per second within 5 times the incident proximity distance, then progressively less often as separation increases.
- Peer telemetry diagnostics now retain source age, tracker transit age, sequence, heading, speed, altitude state, and reporting interval while suppressing repetitive packet and heartbeat messages.
- Reusing a pilot callsign on another active drone now shows an operational warning but no longer prevents confirmation.
- Matrice 4TD clue snapshots now use the controller-aligned camera heading shown by the map field-of-view display, preventing projected clues from landing in the opposite quadrant.
- Submitted clue descriptions retain the useful camera heading, tilt, and timestamp while omitting verbose DJI angle-candidate diagnostics.

Platform-specific changes:
- Android's expired archive-folder confirmation now clearly offers either continuing with the displayed Drone Trax directory or changing the archive directory.
- Android and iOS provide the same ownership-independent peer traffic exchange, map display, adaptive reporting, and bounded diagnostics.

Known platform differences:
- Peer positions are advisory map information in this release. Existing proximity alerts continue to use locally received aircraft telemetry while peer freshness and altitude behavior receive further field validation.
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
