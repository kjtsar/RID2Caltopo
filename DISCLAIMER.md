RID2Caltopo is an aid for search-and-rescue planning, situational awareness,
and review. It is not a certified aviation, navigation, collision-avoidance,
dispatch, or life-safety system.

Remote ID, telemetry, maps, network services, video analysis, anomaly
detections, and other information displayed by the application may be missing,
delayed, inaccurate, incomplete, or unavailable. An anomaly detection is an
area for a trained operator to review, not a confirmation that a person or
other search subject has been found. The absence of a detection does not mean
that an area is clear.

Operators remain responsible for complying with applicable laws, regulations,
incident-command procedures, aircraft operating limitations, and agency
policies. Maintain required visual observation and independently verify
