Latest changes:
- Expire unanswered tracker video and recording-transfer prompts after 60 seconds on Android and iOS, while preserving independently bounded approved sessions.
- Reconcile streams and resume valid approved recording transfers when the tracker connection returns, without asking the operator twice.
- Added ordinary browser downloads for Android and iOS recordings. Transfers require tablet approval unless Remote Video Control is enabled; the tablet original is retained and the tracker's temporary relay copy is deleted after delivery.
- Publish recordings from MediaMTX's file-closed event so new video appears without timing guesses or manual refreshes.
- Keep Android's live source attached during tracker negotiation, treat normal source endings as clean stops, and clear stale tracker request state.
- Stabilized routed bandwidth checks and recorded playback so retries do not force last-resort quality or loop fragmented media.
- Link archived CalTopo tracks to the exact recording by stable session ID, preventing same-drone recordings from collapsing after restart.
- Name new Android and iOS recordings with drone, local date, and start time while retaining legacy-file support.
- Fixed iOS migration so recordings captured earlier today remain available after an update.
- Added persistent Remote Video Control on both platforms, allowing an authenticated requester to use measured, bandwidth-safe quality without a pilot/VO prompt while preserving visible and spoken sharing notices.
- Fixed iPad two-way audio so microphone changes no longer interrupt the negotiated VoIP session.
- Recover interrupted CalTopo LiveTracks into archived tracks after termination, update, or relaunch.
- Base maximum-idle shutdown on launch or the latest RID message, including messages without valid positions.
- Added adjustable clue camera heading and prefer movement-derived course as the initial direction.
- Publish compatible aircraft-motion and camera-link metadata with CalTopo LiveTrack reports.
- Refresh tablet-page thumbnails only while an authenticated viewer is present.
- Preserve gathered iPad WebRTC candidates in one-shot answers and improve routed-connection diagnostics.
- Added short authenticated tablet links for live and recent video with thumbnails.
- Show live streams before drone identity is known; add CalTopo links after matching telemetry.
- Added pilot/VO arbitration for one remote viewer, including declines and priority replacement.
- Added matched live and archived CalTopo video links and thumbnails.
- Added 30 m, 10 m, and maximum 1 m USGS terrain downloads with storage and cache checks.
- Prefer the finest downloaded terrain at each aircraft position for better AGL accuracy.
- Standardized map and video telemetry as ATO, AGL, RNG, and HDG on both platforms.
- Limit Ignore to the drone's current flight so a later flight requests confirmation again.
- Clarified the independent CalTopo Teams API relationship and replaced public organization examples with mySAR.

Platform-specific changes:
- iOS now matches Android's top-bar double-tap shortcut for switching between the Main and Live View screens.
- Android and iOS show the same CalTopo relationship language in the required launch acknowledgment.

Known platform differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files for backup and transfer; Google Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so the Apple app records that value as unavailable.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Person Relevance filtering is not yet available on iOS.
