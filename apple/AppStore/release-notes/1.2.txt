Remaining Android differences:
- Apple uses iCloud Drive and Files rather than automatic Google Drive sync; Drive remains selectable through Files when installed.
- iOS does not expose handset Wi-Fi RSSI, so Apple cannot show Android's controller-strength percentage.
- iOS may suspend video and anomaly processing while locked or backgrounded; Bluetooth restoration is best effort.
- Apple still lacks Android's advanced track/alert tuning, full Proximity Pairs diagnostics, archive cleanup panel, and AD Person Relevance runtime.
